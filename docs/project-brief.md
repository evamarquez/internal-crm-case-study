# Project brief

## Business problem

The team was coordinating daily work through Airtable. Status fields had become workflow stages, filtered views acted as role-specific screens, and important business rules lived in people's heads.

The project was not simply a data move. It was an operating-model change.

## Goal

Create a custom internal CRM and dashboard that made clients, brands, projects, deliverables, ownership, blockers, and status visible for a 12-person team.

## Scope

- Process mapping and discovery
- Target data model
- Operational views and dashboards
- Migration preparation and coexistence
- Scenario validation
- SOPs, known issues, decision records, and handoff

## Constraints

- Private records, client names, internal URLs, and production IDs remain private.
- The Airtable workflow is evidence to interpret, not a schema to copy blindly.
- The team needs daily continuity while the new system is validated.

## Definition of done

- The real workflow and hidden rules are documented.
- Selected production scenarios work in the target system.
- The team can use the CRM daily after rollout.
- Ownership and handoff documentation remain with the operation.
